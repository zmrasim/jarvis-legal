J.A.R.V.I.S.Personal AI assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

JARVIS is a personal, locally installed AI assistant (“JARVIS”, “the app”). This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is handled, and the choices you have. JARVIS is designed for a single user running the app on their own computer.

JARVIS’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information JARVIS accesses

When you choose to connect a Google account, JARVIS requests only the permissions needed for the features you use:

  • Gmail (read and compose): to search and read messages you ask about, and to create drafts. JARVIS does not send email automatically.
  • Google Calendar: to read events and to create or change events you ask for.
  • Google Sheets: to read or update spreadsheets you name.
  • Google Drive: to find files and save documents or reports you ask JARVIS to create.
  • Basic account information: the email address of the connected account, used to show which account is active.

JARVIS does not request access to Google Contacts, Google Photos, or any Google service not listed above. You can decline any permission on Google’s consent screen; features that need a declined permission will not work.

2. How the information is used

Data is used only to carry out requests you make in the app — for example, “what’s on my calendar today?”, “check my email”, “add this to my sheet”, or a mission you explicitly approve. JARVIS does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or any purpose unrelated to your request.

3. Where data is stored

JARVIS runs on your own computer. OAuth access and refresh tokens, settings, notes, and mission records are stored locally in the JARVIS application folder. On Linux and macOS the configuration file is restricted to your user account (mode 600). There is no JARVIS cloud service that stores your Google data.

Requests to Google are made directly from your computer to Google’s APIs. A local server listens only on loopback by default. If you enable private phone access, traffic travels over your own private Tailscale network; JARVIS does not operate a public relay for your Google data.

4. Sharing and disclosure

  • JARVIS does not sell, rent, or trade your Google user data.
  • JARVIS does not share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Data is sent to Google only as part of the API calls needed to fulfil your request.
  • If you configure an optional AI provider (for example DeepSeek, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a local model), the text of your request and the relevant results may be sent to that provider under its own terms. You choose whether to configure such a provider; local models keep the work on your machine.
  • JARVIS may disclose data if required by applicable law, but only data stored locally on your machine is within its control.

5. Retention and deletion

Google data retrieved by JARVIS is kept only as long as needed for the request or in local records you choose to keep (for example, mission notes in your vault). You can delete those records from the app or your filesystem at any time.

To disconnect Google, use the JARVIS tool armory to disconnect the Google tiles, or delete the app’s local token file. You can also revoke JARVIS’s access at any time from your Google Account’s Third-party apps & services page. Revoking access immediately stops new API calls.

6. Security

JARVIS uses Google’s OAuth 2.0 flow and stores tokens locally rather than in a remote database. The local configuration is restricted to the operating-system user. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; you are responsible for securing your computer account and any keys you add.

7. Children

JARVIS is a personal productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. It is not intended for use by children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
